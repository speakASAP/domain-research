# Validation debt

## Purpose
This ledger records known domain-research gaps that are intentionally documented as follow-up items and not treated as current task failures during the IPS onboarding pass.

## Rules
- Only record real issues with a clear owner and closed-loop follow-up path.
- Keep entries factual and scoped to the repo’s actual operational boundary.
- Do not use placeholder markers such as `REPLACE_ME`, `TBD`, or `TASK-XXX`.

## Entries
- Provider purchase flow and production notification recipient policy require explicit owner approval before expansion beyond the current evidence-based domain workflow.

## Update format
- Date: 2026-08-31
- Issue: brief summary
- Owner: repository owner or responsible maintainer
- Status: open or resolved
- Next action: concrete closure step
