# Vision

completeness_level: complete


## One-sentence vision
Make domain discovery and lifecycle monitoring repeatable and evidence-backed so Alfares teams can choose better domain names without guesswork or unmanaged purchase decisions.

## Problem statement
Teams need a reliable way to propose domain candidates, check availability and expiry evidence, and know when a previously watched domain becomes available again. Without a repeatable workflow, domain decisions become slow, ad hoc, and easy to mismanage.

## Target users
- Product owners and service teams establishing names for new work
- Marketing and business stakeholders evaluating brand opportunities
- Internal operators who need evidence-backed availability checks for domain launches

## Core user need
The user needs a trustworthy way to generate candidates, validate them against lifecycle evidence, and monitor watched domains without cross-contaminating the broader platform identity or notification infrastructure.

## Key outcomes
- Domain suggestions are useful, repeatable, and grounded in business context.
- Availability checks are evidence-backed and reviewable.
- Domain watches support recheck-driven notification flows with clear operational boundaries.
- The repo stays honest about where provider, auth, and notification ownership begins and ends.

## Non-goals
- Automatically buying domains without provider approval
- Creating a custom notification delivery system in the repo itself
- Owning the identity provider or payment platform for the broader ecosystem

## Success criteria
- The service provides useful domain suggestions and evidence-backed availability checks.
- Watch-based lifecycle checks remain accurate and reviewable.
- The project remains truthful about provider authorization and notification routing boundaries.
